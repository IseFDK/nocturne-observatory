Behind the atlas

A quiet
experiment.

Nocturne is a small observatory built for a browser. Every world is drawn in real time: surfaces, rings, drifting particles and all.

No photographs. No live astronomy feed. Just three imagined objects, and the pleasure of looking around them.

Built with Three.js and custom shaders. Keyboard: arrows to orbit, + / − to zoom, space to pause, R to reset when the world has focus. Respects reduced-motion preferences.
